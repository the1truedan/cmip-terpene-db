One place for compounds, cultivars, and lab COAs — so charts stop fighting five spellings of the same molecule.

why this exists

Spreadsheets keep lying about chemistry.

The same terpene shows up under five names. A boiling point is a bare number with no source. Aroma wheels get mixed into science columns. CMIP is a design for doing that job once, then sharing it.

identity

One row per real chemical

Everything else points at it. Do not paste α-pinene into ten tables and hope the spellings stay friends.

provenance

Numbers need a story

A boiling point is not just 177. Units, method, citation, and confidence travel with the value.

isomers

α-pinene is not β-pinene

Cis/trans and stereo matter for pathways and effects. Flatten them and the graph is fiction.

layers

Pretty wheels are not chemistry

Aroma, flavor, and effect taxonomies sit beside the science. They do not live inside the compound record.

how it is supposed to stack

Literature in. Charts out. Nothing mixed in the middle.

  → Reference Tables
  → Chemistry
  → Cultivars

  → Retail Products

raw (unchanged sources)
  → staging (parsed, checked)
  → core (compounds, cultivars, measurements, citations)

named sources, not mystery CSVs

Open data first. Models do not invent molecules.

Planned loaders would pull from public databases with licenses written down. Unvetted scrapes stay out.

CIDs, synonyms, InChI, SMILES, formula, mass. Public domain. The seed for a compound registry.

Boiling points and friends with literature citations. Per-record pull, not a bulk scrape.

unverified staging · not science

The scrape pile is withheld. This cloud is just how often words repeat.

Bigger type = more repeats in a local strain/terpene/spectra-filename dump that never got a license review. Not PubChem. Not COAs. Raw tables are not in this repo.

what is deliberately missing

No compounds. No strains. No spectra. No medical advice.

Builders source licensed chemistry themselves. The private staging pile stays private until someone can show a paper trail. This repository is the architecture sketch after that pivot — not a compliance product, not a warehouse, not a chart that pretends a spreadsheet was a database.

why scheduling shows up in a schema repo

Research access changes whether citable chemistry is worth building.

On 18 December 2025 President Trump signed the executive order Increasing Medical Marijuana and Cannabidiol Research, directing the Attorney General to finish rulemaking toward Schedule III.

That is direction to complete a process, not a one-line rewrite of every CSA row. A May 2024 DOJ/DEA proposal and later 2026 DOJ medical-product actions sit on the same track. Precision lives in FEDERAL_CANNABIS_RESCHEDULING_CONTEXT.md.
